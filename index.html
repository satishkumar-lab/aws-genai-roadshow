A hands-on GenAI workshop for technical founders & engineering teams taking AI to production.

About the event

A curated half-day technical event for engineering teams in Mumbai who are actively building - or planning to build - with Generative AI on AWS. This is not a sales pitch or a keynote. It is a working session designed around the real challenges of deploying agentic AI systems in production.

Hosted by AWS and CloudKeeper, the day brings together practitioners and architects to share battle-tested patterns, live architecture walkthroughs, and direct consultation time.

What you'll explore

Three sessions · Half day

SESSION 1

60 min

Using LLMs to Develop Software: SDLC in the Agentic Era

An advanced, practitioner-level session - not an intro to prompting. Agentic coding workflows, AI-native CI/CD patterns, and how top startups are compressing ship cycles with LLM-driven development end-to-end.

SESSION 2

90 min

Build and Deploy AI Agents at Scale

Architecture and operations for production agents - multi-agent orchestration, guardrails, observability, and failure handling patterns that make agentic systems reliable enough to trust.

SESSION 3

15 min

CloudKeeper: FinOps for AI

Know about CloudKeeper, an AWS Premier Partner, and how our FinOps for AI offering leverages AI to identify, optimize, and automate cloud cost savings across your AI workloads.
